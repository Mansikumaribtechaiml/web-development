Jaipur Travel Destination Guide

Welcome to Jaipur, the famous Pink City of Rajasthan. Jaipur is known for its beautiful forts, palaces, colourful markets, traditional culture and delicious Rajasthani food.

1. Attractions

Jaipur has many famous historical attractions. Some of the most popular places include Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jal Mahal and Jantar Mantar.

Learn more about Amber Fort

Hawa Mahal is one of Jaipur's most recognizable landmarks and is known for its distinctive architecture and many windows.

Explore Jal Mahal

2. Culture

Jaipur has a rich Rajasthani culture.
Visitors can experience traditional music, folk dances, colourful clothing, handicrafts and local festivals.
The city is also famous for its traditional markets and textiles.

Popular shopping areas include Johari Bazaar, Bapu Bazaar and Tripolia Bazaar, where visitors can find jewellery, textiles, handicrafts and traditional products.

3. Food

Jaipur is well known for its traditional Rajasthani cuisine.
Visitors can try dishes such as Dal Baati Churma, Kachori, Ghevar, Mirchi Bada and Ker Sangri.

Try traditional Rajasthani food during your visitto experience the local flavours and culinary traditions.

4. Best Time to Visit

The best time to explore Jaipur is generally during the cooler months.
October to March is a comfortable period for sightseeing and exploring outdoor attractions.

During the warmer months, plan outdoor sightseeing during the morning or evening and carry water with you.

5. Travel Tips

Plan your attractions in advance and keep enough time for sightseeing.
Wear comfortable clothes and footwear, especially when visiting forts and large historical complexes.

Always keep drinking water with you and follow the rules of historical monuments.

Jaipur has good road, railway and air connections, making it accessible from many major Indian cities.
