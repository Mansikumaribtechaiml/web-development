Academic Programs

Undergraduate Programs

The university offers undergraduate programs in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Administration and Engineering.

The Computer Science program focuses on programming, databases, web development, artificial intelligence and software engineering.

Students study subjects such as Mathematics1, Mathematics2 and advanced technical subjects. Some scientific concepts may also use expressions such as x2 and H2O.
