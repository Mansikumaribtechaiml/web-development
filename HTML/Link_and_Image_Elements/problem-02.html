College Students Launch a New Green Campus Initiative

Students Come Together to Make Their Campus More Environment-Friendly

A group of college students has launched a new environmental initiative to make their campus cleaner, greener and more sustainable. The campaign was announced during a special student meeting and has received support from teachers and students.

The initiative was launched on 24 August 2026 at the college campus. Students participated in a cleanliness drive and planted trees in different areas of the campus. The event focused on creating awareness about protecting the environment.

More than 200 students participated in the first phase of the campaign. Volunteers collected waste, planted saplings and encouraged other students to reduce the use of plastic on campus. The students said that small actions can create a meaningful change when everyone works together.

The campaign was organised because increasing waste and plastic use have become important concerns. Students believe that a cleaner campus can provide a healthier and more pleasant environment for everyone. The organisers also plan to conduct awareness programmes in the coming weeks.
