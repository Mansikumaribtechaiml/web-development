TechFest 2026

Get ready for the biggest technology celebration of the year! TechFest 2026 brings together students, technology enthusiasts, developers and innovators for an exciting day of learning, creativity and competition.

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a college technology festival designed to give students an opportunity to explore the latest technologies and demonstrate their technical skills. The event will include coding competitions, project exhibitions, workshops and interactive activities.

Highlights

The festival will feature exciting activities including a coding challenge, robotics demonstration, web development competition, project exhibition and technology quiz. Participants will also get opportunities to interact with other students and learn from experts.

Main Announcement: Participants can showcase their innovative projects and compete for exciting prizes and certificates.

Speakers
