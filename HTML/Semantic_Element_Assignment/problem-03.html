How to Request an Appointment

  1. Choose your preferred department.
  2. Select a doctor.
  3. Choose your preferred date and time.
  4. Enter your contact information.
  5. Describe your reason for consultation.
  6. Submit the appointment request.

Consultation Information

Consultation
A scheduled meeting with a healthcare professional for general guidance and discussion.
Follow-up
A later appointment to review progress or continue care.
OPD
OPD stands for OPD, where patients visit for consultation without hospital admission.
Appointment
A request for a specific date and time to meet a doctor.
