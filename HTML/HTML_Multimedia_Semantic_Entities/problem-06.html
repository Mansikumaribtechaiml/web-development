How to Order

  1. Open the official Domino's website.
  2. Select your location.
  3. Choose your favourite food items.
  4. Add the items to your cart.
  5. Select delivery or takeaway where available.
  6. Enter the required order details.
  7. Review your order.
  8. Place the order.
