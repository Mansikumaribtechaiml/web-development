Understanding Savings Accounts

What is a Savings Account?

A savings account is a bank account designed to help customers keep money safely while providing access to banking services.

SBI savings accounts provide facilities such as mobile banking, SMS alerts, internet banking and YONO.

"Save regularly and understand the terms and conditions of the financial products you use."

Deposits Saving

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows customers to place a lump sumfor a selected period and earn interest according to the applicable terms.

SBI provides different deposit products including fixed deposits and recurring deposits.

Recurring Deposit

A recurring deposit allows customers to make regular deposits over a selected period to build savings.

Understanding Loans

Home Loan

A home loan may be used for eligible housing-related purposes. The borrower normally repays the loan through scheduled instalments.

Personal Loan

A personal loan is a type of credit facility offered subject to the bank's eligibility criteria and terms.

SBI provides online application facilities for home loans and personal loans.

Important Financial Tips

  • Prepare a simple monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your available income regularly.
  • Understand interest rates before taking a loan.
  • Compare the total cost of borrowing.
  • Keep important banking information secure.
  • Review your bank statements regularly.
  • Understand fees and charges before using a service.
  • Use official banking websites and apps.
  • Avoid sharing passwords, PINs and OTPs.
  • Ask the bank for clarification when you do not understand a financial product.

Steps for Opening a Bank Account

  1. Select a suitable account.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Keep the required KYC documents ready.
  4. Complete the account-opening application.
  5. Submit the required information and documents.
  6. Complete the applicable verification process.
  7. Receive the account details after successful opening.
  8. Set up the available digital banking services.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest on a depositor loan according to the applicable terms.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a regular payment made towards certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money deposited or borrowed, before applicable interest.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or deposit remains active according to its terms.

Basic EMI Formula

A commonly used EMI calculation involves:

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)N ÷ ((1 + R)N - 1)

Where:

  • P = Principal
  • R = Monthly interest rate
  • N = Number of monthly instalments
