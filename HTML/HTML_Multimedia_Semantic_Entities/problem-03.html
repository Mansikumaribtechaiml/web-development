Welcome to Jaipur

The Pink City of India

Jaipur is one of India's most popular tourist destinations. It is famous for its royal palaces, beautiful forts, colourful markets and rich culture.

Experience history, food, architecture unforgettable memories in the beautiful Pink City.

For more information, visit our travel guide.

Quick Travel Tip

Carry water, comfortable shoes and keep some extra cash important documents while travelling.

One-Day Jaipur Itinerary

  1. Start your day with a visit to Amber Fort.
  2. Visit Jal Mahal on the way back.
  3. Explore City Palace and Jantar Mantar.
  4. Take photographs at Hawa Mahal.
  5. Visit local markets for shopping.
  6. Enjoy sunset from Nahargarh Fort.
