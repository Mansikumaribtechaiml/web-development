Registration Process

  1. Choose the event in which you want to participate.
  2. Check the eligibility and registration requirements.
  3. Fill in the registration form.
  4. Pay the registration fee if applicable.
  5. Submit the form before the deadline.
  6. Receive confirmation from the organizing committee.

Registration Deadline: 5 March 2026

General Fest Rules

  • All participants must carry their college ID card.
  • Participants must report to the venue on time.
  • Maintain discipline throughout the event.
  • Follow the instructions given by the coordinators.
  • Any unfair practice may lead to disqualification.
  • Respect other participants and organizers.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event in which eligible students can participate individually.
Team Event
An event where participants compete as a group or team.
Individual Event
A competition where one student participates independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing and organizing an event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where winners are decided.
