Steps Before Attending a Practical Class

  1. Check the practical timetable.
  2. Bring your lab manual.
  3. Complete the required preparation.
  4. Attend the practical class on time.
  5. Submit your practical work to the faculty.

Academic Rules

  • Attendance is mandatory.
  • Students must reach class on time.
  • Mobile phones should remain silent during class.
  • Maintain discipline inside the classroom.
  • Assignments must be submitted before the deadline.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A class where the faculty teaches theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or coding.
Tutorial
A smaller learning session for solving problems and clearing doubts.
Internal Assessment
Marks given through tests, assignments and classroom performance.
Practical
Hands-on work performed in a laboratory or practical environment.
